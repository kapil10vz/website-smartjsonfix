How to fix broken JSON in four steps

Average time to repair: under 30 seconds.

  1. Paste your malformed JSON into the Raw Input panel.
  2. Click Fix & Format. SmartJSONFix highlights the error line and explains it.
  3. Review the corrected output in the Formatted Result panel.
  4. Copy the fixed JSON, or click Save to keep the snippet locally for later.

Frequently asked questions

Is SmartJSONFix free?

Yes — 100% free. No signup, no paywall, no rate-limit gate.

Is my JSON data uploaded to a server?

No. SmartJSONFix runs entirely in your browser. The JSON you paste never leaves your device.

Can SmartJSONFix repair invalid JSON automatically?

Yes. It auto-repairs trailing commas, single quotes, unquoted keys, Python literals, and bracket balancing.

Does SmartJSONFix work on mobile?

Yes — desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers fully supported.
